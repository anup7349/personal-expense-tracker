# EarnTrack Play Store Notes

EarnTrack is a small offline money tracker for daily earners, freelancers, students, riders, and small shop owners. It asks for no permissions, has no ads, includes local sign-in/registration, and stores users plus income/expense entries in an on-device SQLite database.

## Upload file

After building, upload the signed Android App Bundle:

`app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`

Package name:

`com.anups.earntrack`

## Privacy

Data collected: none by the developer. Username, password, and money entries are stored locally on the user's device only.

Data shared: none.

Suggested short description:

`Track today’s income, expenses, and profit privately on your phone.`

Suggested full description:

`EarnTrack helps you quickly record income and expenses so you always know how much money you made today, this month, and this year. It is built for small daily earners, freelancers, students, riders, and shop owners who want a simple private money log. The app works offline, asks for no permissions, shows no ads, and stores account plus money records locally on the device.`
